Quotation & Citation Tags

Six semantic HTML tags, each styled so its role is easy to tell apart.

<q> — short inline quotation

The browser adds quotation marks automatically. Here, it is styled in italic rust-orange text.

As Alan Kay said, The best way to predict the future is to invent it.

<blockquote> — long quotation from another source

The power of the Web is in its universality. Access by everyone regardless of disability is an essential aspect.

— Tim Berners-Lee, in W3C Accessibility Initiative

Block-level, indented, with a left bar and tinted background.

<cite> — title of a work

Referenced works get a blue underlined style, upright, not italic: The Art of Computer Programming by Donald Knuth, or Fake News Detection Using Machine Learning.

<q> vs <cite> side by side

<q>
Words someone said: Simplicity is prerequisite for reliability.
<cite>
Name of the work: Structured Programming

<abbr> — abbreviation or acronym

Hover over HTML, CSS or W3C to see the full form.
